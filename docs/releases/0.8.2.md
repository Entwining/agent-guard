# v0.8.2

`glab config get` is refused for token keys, as `glab auth status --show-token` already was. This covers `token` and its aliases `gitlab_token` and `oauth_token`, `job_token`, `oauth2_refresh_token` and `refresh_token`, wherever `-g` or `--host` appears in the command. Other keys, such as `host` and `user`, stay readable, and `glab auth status` without token-display flags still reports whether glab is authenticated.

Reading glab's `config.yml`, where glab keeps its tokens in plaintext when it has no keyring, is refused as a credential-file read at `~/.config/glab-cli/config.yml` and `~/Library/Application Support/glab-cli/config.yml`, including globs in those directories and searches rooted at them. Under another `glab-cli` directory, such as a custom `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, only a literal path to `config.yml` is refused; the [known limits](https://github.com/Entwining/agent-guard/blob/v0.8.2/docs/setup.md#known-limits) list this. `aliases.yml` beside it stays readable.
